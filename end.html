Final Analysis

How did Piggy change throughout the novel?

Piggy at the beginning mostly just observes, unable to do much without being bullied by Jack. Here he uses logic, reasoning, and rules to help anchor himself to the civilization they left behind. Once Jack breaks his glasses however, he stops trying to "organize a society", and instead just focuses on surviving the island. Here he starts to become more and more dependent on Ralph for physical protection, while providing him with intellectual ideas. At the end however, Piggy completely changes this up and finds a spark through a combination of anger and morality. Despite being unable to see, his internal vision of right and wrong has become clearer to him, leading him to make a final stand, where he pits his logic against Jack's savagery. This ultimately fails and ends up leading to his death.

What psychological forces influenced their behavior?

One thing that comes into play a lot is the Super Ego. Piggy represents the conscience of the group, representing everything they left behind and the little morality they might have left. He tries to use the rules of society, even when those rules can't be backed up by physical power. Another important psychological force is Social Exclusion and Scapegoating. Piggy's superior intellect and weak physical strength, he turns into the "target" for all of the groups anger and frustration. This pressure shapes his character on the island, and he finds it harder and harder to find strength for logic and organization as he gets pushed around more and more.

What does your character suggest about human nature?

One thing Piggy suggests is that while logic and science are extremely powerful tools, without the rules and backing of civilization, they quickly fall apart when the group follows their primal instincts and starts to ignore the "voice of reason". Piggy also displays that some people have an innate need for order. While most of the kids on the island have fun and enjoy their "freedom", Piggy uses his intellect to help explain and organize the different things that happen on the island. He also highlights the fact that the human mind does include a natural desire for justice. While most of the kids give in to their Id and become savages, Piggy rises above them and becomes the Super Ego, trying to keep their island civilization from collapsing.
