An Analysis of Piggy's Behavior

These are my findings on the psychology behind Piggy's actions in Lord of the Flies.

The Conch

The Conch is one of the greatest organizational tools in the book, and Piggy was able to recognize it's value right from the start!

Piggy's Glasses

Piggy's Glasses are one of the many symbols that readers can look at to see how the island is going. As the glasses start to break, their "Coral Island" starts to fall apart.

The Super Ego

Piggy is the Super Ego because he act as the "conscience" of the group. He goes to logic and science to explain things and tries to stop them from becoming savages.
