Power Struggle

Piggy's Behavior of Note:

After the "beastie" gets spotted on the mountain, all of the boys are too scared to go back up the mountain and light the fire. Piggy, being the logical one in the group, comes up with the simple idea of moving the fire down by where the bathing pool is located.


"But what's wrong with a fire down here? A fire could be built on them rocks. On the sand even." (Golding 129).

Psychological Interpretation of Piggy's Behavior:

In Jack's absence around Ralph's group, Piggy feels more free to help out with the group. He doesn't feel like he's just Ralph's advisor anymore; he feels like he's a problem solver. Without getting bullied and pushed around by Jack, Piggy is able to start feeling more capable and able to help out around the island.


Here we can see an example of Self-Efficacy. Piggy's ability to believe in himself, knowing that he could solve this problem, is exactly what allowed him to solve the problem. This successful idea of his will also boost his confidence so he can contribute more later, and his status within the group.

My Prediction for how Piggy will act in the Future:

Around Ralph's group, Piggy will start to act more vocal, more confident, and help out more around the island. One thing he needs to watch out for though is to make sure to not let his newfound pride make him forget how dangerous Jack and his group still are.
